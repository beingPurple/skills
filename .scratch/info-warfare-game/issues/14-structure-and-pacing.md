# 14 — Structure & pacing

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

What is the campaign shape? Define:

- Number of **cycles/days**, and whether play is **episodic** (discrete news events) or continuous.
- **Escalation** over the campaign.
- **Save/checkpoint** model, playthrough length, and where routes visibly **diverge** in the timeline.
