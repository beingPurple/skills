# 02 — Faction roster: the parties and their policy pros/cons

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

## Question

Who are the parties/factions vying to influence the nation, how many, and what does each want? For each faction define:

- Its ideology and the concrete **policy** it pushes.
- The **pros and cons** of that policy for everyday life (the trade-offs that make routes interesting).
- What it **offers and threatens** the player (leaks, money, protection / pressure, ruin).

Design goal: a set where no faction is obviously "right", so aligning with any of them costs something. How many factions best serve the routes and endings we want?
