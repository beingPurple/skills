# 06 — The story object & the publish verbs

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

What is a "story" as a data object, and what verbs can the player apply to one? Candidate verbs: publish / spike / bury / edit-the-headline / spin / delay / fact-check / amplify. Define:

- The **story schema** — does a story carry a hidden truth value, a source/faction, a slant dial, a reach estimate, a decay timer?
- The **verb set** — the complete vocabulary of actions the player can take on a story.

Everything downstream (consequences, factions, bots) manipulates this vocabulary, so it must be pinned precisely.
