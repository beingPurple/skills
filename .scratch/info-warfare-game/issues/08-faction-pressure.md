# 08 — Faction pressure mechanics

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 06

## Question

How do factions act **on** the player between cycles? Leaks/scoops offered, bribes, ad revenue, protection, threats, planted stories, demands to spike a story. Define:

- The **reputation/relationship representation** per faction.
- What happens at its **extremes** — patronage on one end, retaliation on the other.
- How pressure is delivered without becoming more reading than the player has time for.
