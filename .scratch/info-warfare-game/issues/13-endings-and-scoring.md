# 13 — Endings & scoring

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 11, 07

## Question

What are the endings and how does the game decide which you get? Define:

- The **ending set** (at least one per route, plus failure states).
- The **scoring/threshold logic** over the state variables that selects an ending.
- The **epilogue form** — the fate of the nation, of you, of the factions.

How many endings, and how legible should the path to each be to a first-time player?
