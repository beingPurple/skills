# 07 — Consequence model: how choices move the nation

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 02

## Question

How do publishing choices move the nation? Define:

- The tracked **state variables** (public opinion per faction, trust in your outlet, unrest, the government's grip, your finances?).
- How a **published story changes** them.
- How the player **sees** the consequence (next-day headlines, on-screen meters, world events).

This is the engine that turns individual choices into routes — it must be legible enough to play around, opaque enough to surprise.
