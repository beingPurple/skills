# 01 — Setting bible: the fantasy nation & the greater world

Type: grilling
Status: open

## Question

What fantasy nation is the game set in, and what surrounds it? Pin down:

- The nation: name, government type, and who currently holds power.
- The **inciting crisis** that makes *this* news cycle the one worth playing (an election, a war, a succession, an economic collapse?).
- The **greater world**: neighbouring powers / geopolitics that the factions ultimately answer to.
- The crucial fusion question: **how do fantasy elements (magic, nonhuman peoples, gods, old powers) coexist with a modern Internet + bot media ecosystem?** Is magic the "technology", does tech sit alongside magic, or is the Internet itself something stranger here?

Deliver a one-page setting bible plus a seed glossary of in-world media/tech terms.
