# 05 — Genre & real-world information-warfare research

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

Survey comparable games (The Republia Times, Headliner, Orwell, Papers Please, Yes Your Grace, We The Revolution) and real-world information-warfare / disinformation concepts (agenda-setting, gatekeeping, the propaganda model, troll/bot farms, astroturfing, coordinated inauthentic behaviour). Extract:

- Which mechanics create the "too much to read, too little time" tension.
- How consequence and branching toward multiple endings get modelled.
- Real-world concepts that could become game mechanics.
- Pitfalls to avoid.

## Answer

Resolved by the `/research` subagent fired during charting. Full brief: [`../research/05-genre-and-infowar.md`](../research/05-genre-and-infowar.md).

Key findings:

- **Direct ancestor — The Republia Times:** countdown-to-press clock + a front-page grid too small for the incoming wire feed; dual Loyalty/Readership meters; hidden rebel line; a coerced state-media ending. This is our skeleton.
- **Papers, Please** is the pressure + branching benchmark: deliberately slow interface, escalating rules, ~20 flag-gated endings, "where are they now" epilogues that make abstract choices bite.
- **Yes, Your Grace** = cleanest "demand always exceeds capacity" loop. **We. The Revolution** = the editor→operator escalation via a separate intrigue metagame + multi-faction favour.
- **Westport Independent / Orwell:** the richest verb is not accept/reject but **reframe** — headline toggles, paragraph-striking, partial-truth "datachunks" where two true facts imply a lie.
- **Anti-pattern — Headliner: NoviNews:** telegraphed binary no-nuance choices kill all tension; you know the outcome before you pick. Also: don't delay consequences to late-game.
- **Real-world mechanics to mine:** agenda-setting → a per-issue **Salience** meter; gatekeeping → the finite kill/hold/run gate; framing → per-story **slant** selector; Herman–Chomsky five filters → a sponsor/ownership/official-source constraint layer that quietly captures you; firehose-of-falsehood + astroturfing + coordinated inauthentic behaviour → the operator-mode bot network (output/authenticity/heat) countered by a rising **Exposure** meter.

**Headline recommendation:** make **framing** (not accept/reject) the core expressive verb, and **decouple it from probabilistic / delayed / partial-truth outcomes so the player is sometimes wrong.** This fixes Headliner's telegraphing flaw, powers a no-golden-ending consequence model, and lets the **hidden state-media route emerge from convenience** (leaning on free official sources + sponsor-safe framings) rather than an explicit order.
