# 10 — Bots & the editor→operator escalation

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 07

## Question

How does the Editor escalate into an information-**operator**? Define:

- The **toolkit** — amplification, sockpuppets, manufactured trends, coordinated leaks, bot swarms.
- How it is **acquired** (a faction hands it over? the state? you build it?).
- What wielding it **costs** — credibility, exposure risk, moral weight, escalating enemies.

This is the hinge between the newsroom origin and the darker routes, including state-media.
