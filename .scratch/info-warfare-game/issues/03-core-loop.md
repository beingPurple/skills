# 03 — Core loop: read-under-pressure → publish

Type: prototype
Status: open

## Question

How does the moment-to-moment loop *feel*? Stories arrive faster than they can be read; the player triages under a clock and decides what to run. Pin down:

- The **unit of time** (a shift? a news cycle? a day?).
- How **time pressure is expressed** (a ticking clock, a filling inbox, a deadline, all of these).
- What the player **can read** (headline only vs. skim vs. full story at a time cost) — the reading economy.
- The **decision surface** at the moment of choice.

"How should it behave" is the key question — build a rough playable slice (paper/text prototype via /prototype) to feel whether the tension is fun before locking it.
