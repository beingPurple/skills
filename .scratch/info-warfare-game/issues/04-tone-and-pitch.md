# 04 — Tone, pitch & the player fantasy

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

What is the tone and the one-sentence pitch? Grim political thriller, biting satire, dark comedy, earnest civic drama — or a deliberate blend? Deliver:

- A **one-line pitch**.
- The **fantasy statement**: "You are ___, every day you ___, and it matters because ___."
- 2–3 **tonal touchstones** (games, films, books) that set the register.

This steers the writing of headlines, factions, and endings, so it is cheap to settle early and expensive to leave vague.

## Answer

### One-line pitch

> You edit a newspaper in a fantasy nation coming apart at the seams — and every day brings
> more to read than you have time for. The stories you skim are the ones that decide who rules.
> A heightened, operatic thriller about drowning in the news cycle until complicity feels like
> just hitting your deadline.

### Fantasy statement

> **You are** the editor of the last paper anyone still trusts in a fracturing fantasy nation;
> **every day you** triage far more stories than you could ever read and choose how to frame the
> few that run; **and it matters because** the nation reads you to know what is true — and slowly
> becomes whatever you let it believe.

### Emotional target (route-dependent — this is the important nuance)

- **Default, across most routes: overwhelmed → complicit.** The dominant feeling is the crush of
  the wire feed, and the quiet horror of realising your reasonable, deadline-driven choices built
  something monstrous. **Bad endings are *earned by not staying informed and not reading
  critically*** — shallow reading is the sin the game punishes, in slow motion, and makes you own.
- **Specific "power" routes: clever / potent.** On the deliberate-influence paths (the
  editor→operator escalation and the *overt* state-media route) the register shifts to the
  puppet-master rush — reading the board, timing the leak, getting away with it. This shift is a
  *reward the routes grant*, not the baseline.

### Register

- **Heightened & operatic.** Factions, magic, and stakes are vivid and larger-than-life; the
  world does real narrative work rather than sitting as thin realism.
- **Sharp, comic, exaggerated — but never cheap.** Comedy is seasoning that earns the bleak turns.
  This is an operatic political thriller *with satirical bite*, **not** a straight satire (which
  would cheapen the complicity the game is built to make you feel).

### Tonal touchstones

- **Papers, Please** — complicity manufactured by deadline pressure and small compromises.
- **The Republia Times** — the coerced-press framing and dual-meter tension (our skeleton, per 05).
- **Disco Elysium** — heightened, literate, funny-and-devastating; how to be comic without being cheap.
- **Network / The Thick of It** — sharp, exaggerated media-politics that still draws blood.

### Cross-cutting constraints this decision hands downstream

- **Core loop (03) & player knowledge (09):** careless reading must be *tempting and
  consequential* — the punishment-for-not-reading-critically thesis is a hard design constraint,
  not flavour.
- **Routes (11) & state-media (12):** the power routes must *feel different* (clever/potent), not
  merely branch mechanically. The tonal shift is part of their reward.
- **Setting bible (01):** the world may be vivid, strange, operatic — lean in.
- **Faction roster (02):** factions are exaggerated and comic, but their stakes stay real.
